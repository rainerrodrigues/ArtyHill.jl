Example: cycloidal drive, from geometry to controller

examples/cycloidal_drive.jl walks through the whole prototyping loop for a robot joint with a cycloidal reducer.

  1. Geometry → model. The reduction ratio and the output backlash come from the reducer design: CycloidCore.backlash(DriveParams()) from cycloidal-generator when it is installed (9:1, 82.5 arcmin for the default Ø80 mm design), otherwise from the same precomputed numbers.

  2. Simulation. Motor → backlash + compliance → load with viscous and Coulomb friction, as a NonlinearPlant, run through run_realtime_loop! with an SPSCRing recording telemetry.

  3. Identification from telemetry.

    • Backlash from the hysteresis between the motor and load encoders under a slow triangle motion: 84.8 arcmin identified vs 82.5 from the geometry. The difference is the elastic wind-up of the transmission.
    • Inertias, damping and friction by least squares on the energy balance of the two masses, N·u = Jm·N²·φ̈m + bm·N²·φ̇m + JL·θ̈l + bL·θ̇l + Fc·sgn(θ̇l). Adding the motor and load equations cancels the gear torque, so this holds inside and outside the backlash. All five parameters come back within 2 %.

  4. Controller sweep. For ωn = 25, 40 and 60 rad/s, PD gains are computed from the identified model, and two feedback structures follow the same 1 s minimum-jerk move:

    ωn [rad/s]load-side PD (RMS / p-p, mrad)motor-side PD + backlash compensation
    251.64 / 1.731.35 / 1.75
    400.09 / 0.190.27 / 0.20
    603.28 / 10.45 (hunting)0.34 / 0.01

    Load-side feedback puts the backlash inside the loop. It can be excellent, as at ωn = 40, but it hunts at higher bandwidth. Motor-side feedback keeps the backlash outside the loop and stays quiet across the sweep, at the cost of a small static offset that only the load encoder could remove. The example reports these numbers rather than declaring a winner: the right structure depends on your bandwidth and accuracy targets.

Run it with:

julia --threads=2 --project=examples examples/cycloidal_drive.jl

The test suite runs the same script and checks the identification accuracy.
